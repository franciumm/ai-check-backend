Privacy Policy for AI Fighter

Last updated: September 28, 2026

1. Data Collection and Usage

The AI Fighter Chrome Extension only collects and processes data necessary to fulfill its single purpose: detecting AI-generated images. When you use the "Local Scan" feature, the image is processed entirely on your device and never leaves your browser. When you explicitly click "Deep Scan", the URL of the image you clicked is securely transmitted to our backend to be analyzed by the Sightengine API.

2. Website Content

We do not monitor your web browsing history. The extension only accesses the specific images on a website that you explicitly choose to scan.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third parties for advertising or unrelated purposes. The only third-party service involved is Whop (for processing license key payments) and Sightengine (for performing the Deep Scan AI analysis).

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach out via the extension's support page on the Chrome Web Store.
